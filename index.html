B2B self-ordering kiosk, food courts

Self-ordering kiosk for food courts

A self-ordering platform that restaurant brands buy for their food courts. I redesigned the whole flow: item selection, add-ons, payment and receipt printing. The receipt printer sits well below eye level, and the first success screen showed only an arrow, so people walked off without their receipt. Now the screen shows the receipt printing, with an arrow leading down to the slot, and people pick it up straight away.

How I work

A beautiful screen that people can’t use is still broken. Every project starts with the people using it, and ends with code that matches the design.

  1. 01

    Understand

    User interviews, personas, journey maps and a heuristic review of what exists today, so we fix the real problem.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Information architecture, task flows and wireframes — tested with users before anything gets polished.

  3. 03

    Design

    High-fidelity UI, prototypes, micro-interactions and design systems that keep growing products consistent.

  4. 04

    Ship

    Accessible, semantic front-end code or developer-ready specs, then design QA so what launches is what we designed.

Common questions

Short answers for recruiters, hiring managers and clients.

Who is Prabhu Natarajan?

Prabhu Natarajan is a UX/UI designer and HFI Certified Usability Architect (CUA)™ based in Chennai, India. He has designed for the web since 2005, most recently as Assistant Manager, UX/UI in Samsung’s Digital e-Commerce Division from 2016 to 2026.

Is Prabhu available for new work?

Yes. He’s available for full-time UX/UI roles and freelance projects. The quickest way to reach him is by email at me@prabhunatarajan.com.

What does Prabhu do as a UX/UI designer?

He works across the whole process: user research, personas, journey mapping and heuristic reviews, then information architecture, wireframes and prototypes, then high-fidelity UI and design systems, and finally accessible front-end code or developer-ready specs with design QA.

Which industries has he designed for?

eCommerce, fintech, mortgage, healthcare, real estate, edtech and government lending. His work includes Samsung.com/us checkout, the Nebuloan mortgage workspace and the SBA COVID-19 disaster loan application.

Does Prabhu write front-end code?

Yes. He writes semantic, accessible HTML, CSS/SCSS and JavaScript, builds WCAG-compliant interfaces, and can work in Angular, React or Vue codebases when the build drifts from the design.

Can he work with teams outside India?

Yes. He works from Chennai on Indian Standard Time (UTC+5:30) and has designed with teams in the US, the Gulf, Europe and Asia.

What freelance work does Prabhu take on?

UX audits and usability reviews of an existing product, redesigns of key flows such as sign-up, checkout and long forms, design systems, and accessible, WCAG-compliant front-end builds. He can take on one part of a product or the whole journey, from research to shipped UI.

How does a freelance project with Prabhu start?

Email me@prabhunatarajan.com with a few lines about the product, who uses it, what isn’t working and when you need it. That’s enough for a first conversation about scope and approach.

What does a client get at the end of a project?

Whatever your team needs to build from: Figma designs and prototypes with developer-ready specs, or production-ready HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Accessibility is designed in from the start, not added at the end.
